Infrastructure rules and general engineering guidelines, so every Enjab tool ships
production-ready and consistent: security, deployment, pipelines, and more. We publish these
incrementally; each one lands here and in the [changelog](/docs/changelog/standards).

Every standard published here is **mandatory** for every Enjab tool, a hard gate before
production, not a suggestion. They are strict on purpose: they are what keeps a fleet of
tools safe, reliable, and consistent to operate.
